What happens next

Four steps, and none of them are automated.

  1. You write, with three answersThe button above opens a message with three questions already in it: how long you have trained, what you train on and how often, and what you use now. Answer them in one line each. That is the whole application.
  2. A person reads itNot a form handler and not a queue. The test is small on purpose, because a coaching engine is only worth testing against people whose training we can actually follow.
  3. You get a TestFlight invitation, or an honest noIf the build is not right for how you train yet, we say that instead of adding you to a list and going quiet.
  4. You are asked for one thingRate your effort honestly after each set. Round up to look strong and the coach learns a fake version of you, which helps nobody and costs you months of training.

What you are agreeing to

Nothing that needs a checkbox.

Your training is computed on your device. There is no account, so there is no name, email or password of yours held by us, and nothing to delete later except the app. What does and does not leave your phone, including the six things that could and the one of those we have removed, is set out in the privacy policy.

Your email address exists in one place, which is the message you sent, in an inbox. It is not in a mailing tool, it is not in a customer database, and it is not synced anywhere. If you would rather it were not there at all, say so in the message and it goes.

The app is in testing on iPhone. It is not on the App Store yet. While it is in testing everything is open, so testers have the full vault, the exports and the weekly report at no cost. When it ships, the price is 199 US dollars per year, or 29.99 per month, and the accuracy record is published before anyone is charged.
