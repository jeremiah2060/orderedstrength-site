Two different claims

What a receipt proves, and what only the anchor can.

Was it edited afterwards?

ANSWERABLE TODAY

A receipt carries the prediction, a random nonce, and a fingerprint taken over both. Recompute it and you know whether a single character moved. You can do that right now on the verifier, or break one yourself on the home page.

Was it really made beforehand?

NOT YET ANSWERABLE

That needs a timestamp we do not control. Publishing one root hash per day to a public repository gives it: the commit dates come from GitHub, not from us. Until the first anchor lands, we say so rather than implying a proof we do not have.

Why it starts empty

Backfilling would destroy the only thing this is for.

We could generate anchors for every prediction already sealed on a phone and publish them today. The page would look established. It would also be worthless, because a record assembled after the results were known proves nothing about when anything was decided.

So the record starts on the day it starts, and the gap before it is stated rather than hidden. That is the whole product in one decision.
