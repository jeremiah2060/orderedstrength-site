The subscription, in plain numbers

OrderedStrength Pro costs 199 US dollars per year or 29.99 US dollars per month. The yearly plan works out at about 16.58 a month, which is why it exists. Prices are set in App Store Connect and Apple shows you the exact amount in your own currency before you confirm.

A free trial, where one is offered, is stated on the purchase screen with its exact length before you agree to anything. If you have already used a trial on one plan you are not eligible for another on the other plan, which is Apple's rule and not ours.

The subscription renews automatically. Apple charges the same card each period until you cancel. It renews within 24 hours of the period ending unless you cancel at least 24 hours before that. You cancel in your own Apple account settings, not here and not by asking us, and we cannot cancel it for you because we never see your payment details.

Cancelling stops the next charge. It does not shorten the period you already paid for, and it does not delete your training, which lives on your phone either way. Refunds are handled by Apple under their own policy, and if you have a problem getting one, write to us and we will help you chase it.

If we ever change the price, you are told before the change applies to you and you can cancel before it does.

What the subscription does and does not unlock

Free, and staying free: logging, your plan, Jerry's spoken voice, and the accuracy record including every call he got wrong. The leading surfaces of every analysis category, so each one keeps its most useful readouts. Exporting your recent training. Your first import from another app, because charging at the door is charging to be let in.

What Pro opens: the full analytical vault rather than the leading surfaces of each category, your complete history export back to the first set, importing another training log again after the first one, and the weekly report as a file you can send.

Nothing is hidden without saying so. Where the vault is showing you part of a category, the number of remaining surfaces is on the screen. If this list changes, this paragraph changes in the same release, and anything you could do before you paid stays available.

OrderedStrength is not a medical device, not a diagnosis, and not a treatment plan. It does not know your medical history, and it cannot see you. Lifting weights carries real risk of injury.

You are responsible for deciding whether a prescribed load is safe for you on the day. If something hurts, if you are managing an injury or a health condition, or if you are pregnant or recovering from surgery, a qualified healthcare professional decides, not the app and not us. Stop if something is wrong. The app is built to be argued with, and telling it you are hurt changes what it asks of you.

What the coaching is, and is not

Every load the app prescribes is a model's best estimate from the sets you logged and from population research, and the app labels which of the two it is using at the time. It is explicitly not a guarantee of a result. There is no results dataset on this site yet because the test group has not trained long enough to have one, and until there is, nobody should read a number here as a promise about their own body.
