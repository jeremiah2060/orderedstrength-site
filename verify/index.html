Verifier

Check a receipt.

Paste a receipt copied from OrderedStrength. This checks whether the prediction inside it is byte for byte the one that was sealed before the set was performed. No account, no sign in, and nothing you paste is stored.

What this proves, and what it does not

A receipt carries a prediction, a random nonce, and a SHA-256 fingerprint committed before the set. The verifier recomputes the fingerprint from the prediction and the nonce. If they match, the prediction was not edited afterwards. If a single character changed, the fingerprint cannot match.

It does not prove when the commitment was made. That needs a published daily anchor, which does not exist yet. Until it does, this page proves the prediction was not rewritten and says nothing about the timestamp. That limit is stated here rather than left for you to discover. Progress on the anchor is on the record.
