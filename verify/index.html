Verify a receipt

Paste a receipt copied from OrderedStrength. This checks whether the prediction inside it is byte-for-byte the one that was sealed before the set was performed.

What this actually proves, and what it does not

A receipt carries a prediction, a random nonce, and a SHA-256 digest committed before the set. This page recomputes the digest from the prediction and the nonce. If they match, the prediction was not edited after the fact. If a single character changed, the digest cannot match.

It does not prove when the commitment was made. That needs a published daily anchor, which does not exist yet. Until it does, this page proves the prediction was not rewritten, and says nothing about the timestamp. That limit is stated here rather than left for you to discover.
