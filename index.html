Why this is not the same as every other app

Four products, one shared habit

Logging apps record and decide nothing. Plan apps hand you a program that freezes the moment the session starts. Wearables watch your recovery and never write your workout. Chatbots sound fluent and give you a different answer if you ask twice.

All four ask you a question and then ignore the answer. They ask about your injury and program around nothing. They ask what equipment you own and prescribe a machine you do not have. Lifters rarely complain about this. They quietly load a different weight than the one on the screen, and stop believing the rest of it.

The part competitors cannot copy

Every number says where it came from

On day one Jerry has averages for someone your age, sex and weight, and he says so in those words. As you log real sets he shifts off the averages onto your own body, and shows you how far he has moved and how sure he is. The pace follows your evidence, never a countdown, and he never claims to know you after a fixed number of sessions.

The day it matters most

Tell him something hurts and he keeps coaching you

This is where other apps go quiet. Jerry removes what is unsafe, trains everything else, and brings the injured movement back gradually rather than all at once. Pain above the clinical line holds the ramp where it is instead of letting the calendar advance it. The screen shows the honest number of days until full load, and it is the same number the engine is using.

He is built never to shame you. The words missed, skipped and streak are blocked from his coaching by an automatic check, so a rough week cannot trigger a punishment.

Your data

Easy to leave, hard to replace

Your training is computed on your device by our own engines. There is no third party code in this app, no vendor SDK, and no advertising identifier.

One thing does leave your phone, and we would rather write it here than let you find it. Once a day the app sends a small usage beacon. It carries counts and yes or no facts about the app itself: whether you opened it, whether you finished a session, whether you reached the subscription screen, how many times it crashed, and your language. It is tied to a random identifier created on your device that links to nothing about you, and it is cleared when you clear your data. It never carries a set, a weight, a repetition, or anything about your body. There is a switch to turn it off in Data and Privacy, and it works.

Bring your history in from Strong or Hevy so switching does not mean starting at zero, and export your whole record as a single file whenever you want. Nothing about your training is held hostage to keep you subscribed. If you stay, we want it to be because nothing else understands you as well.
